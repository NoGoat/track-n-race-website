Privacy Policy

Covers the Track N Race Android app, Desktop app and website.

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

Track N Race ("Track N Race," "we," "us," or "our") is an open-source, local-first telemetry dashboard and session recording project designed for use with racing simulation software and compatible devices.

This Privacy Policy applies to the following:

Android App
The Track N Race mobile application for Android.
Desktop App
The Track N Race desktop applications for Windows, macOS and Linux, including the Electron, Qt and Minimal builds.
Website
The Track N Race website at track-n-race.com.

The Android App and the Desktop App are referred to together as the "Apps." The Apps, together with the Website, are referred to as the "Services."

The Apps are designed to operate without a backend server or cloud-based data processing infrastructure. We do not operate a service that collects telemetry, account information, or other personal information from the Apps. Communications required for their core functionality take place between compatible devices on the user's local network. The limited exceptions, such as optional update checks in the Desktop App, are described in this Policy.

The Website collects a small amount of anonymous usage information, described in Section 6.

By using the Services, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information the Apps Process

The Apps do not operate a backend collection system and do not intentionally collect or transmit users' personal information to the developer.

The Apps may process the following information locally or exchange it with compatible devices on your local network to provide their functionality.

2.1 Racing Telemetry

The Apps receive and process racing simulation telemetry, which may include:

  • Vehicle speed, gear, throttle, and brake inputs.
  • Tyre temperatures and other vehicle parameters.
  • Lap times, session information, and timing data.
  • Vehicle position and other simulation-provided information, which can include the in-game names of other participants in a session.

This information is processed to display dashboard instruments, graphs, timing information, and other supported features. Where applicable, telemetry is exchanged between compatible devices on the user's local network.

2.2 Device Identification Information

During device pairing, the Android App may transmit a randomly generated device identifier and the device's model name to the Desktop App over the local network. This allows the Desktop App to identify and display the device for pairing and connection management.

This information is not transmitted to a Track N Race-operated backend or external analytics service.

2.3 Locally Stored Information

Depending on the features you use, the Apps may store information on your device or computer, including:

  • Application preferences and configuration.
  • Pairing information and reconnection credentials for paired devices.
  • Optional telemetry session recordings.
  • Files you choose to create, such as spreadsheet exports or screenshots.
  • Diagnostic logs and crash dumps (Desktop App, see Section 4.3).
  • Other data necessary for the Apps to function.

This information remains under your control, subject to file system permissions, operating system behavior, and any actions you take to export or share it.

3. Android App Permissions

The Android App requests permissions necessary to support its features. Permissions may vary by Android version and application release.

3.1 Camera Access

Permission android.permission.CAMERA
Purpose Camera access is used to scan a QR code displayed by the Desktop App for device pairing.
Data handling Camera access is used for real-time QR code recognition. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not intended to be saved as photographs or video recordings or transmitted over a network by the Android App.

You may revoke camera permission through Android settings. QR-based pairing will not be available while camera access is denied, although other functionality may remain available.

3.2 Network and Wi-Fi Access

Permissions android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, and any applicable local-network permissions supported by the targeted Android version.
Purpose Network access supports receiving racing simulation telemetry over the local network (including UDP packets on the configured port, which defaults to 20777 where applicable), discovering compatible Desktop Apps through mDNS/DNS-SD, and exchanging telemetry, pairing information, and control messages with a paired Desktop App over local WebSocket connections.
Data handling The Android App is designed to exchange operational data between compatible devices on the user's local network. It does not intentionally transmit telemetry or pairing information to an external Track N Race backend, cloud service, advertising network, or analytics service.

The Android App's network functionality depends on the configuration of the user's network and compatible devices.

3.3 Wake Lock

Permission android.permission.WAKE_LOCK
Purpose The Android App may use a wake lock or an appropriate Wi-Fi performance lock, where supported, to reduce power-saving interruptions that could affect network reliability during an active session.

This permission is used to support the Android App's operation and is not used to collect personal information.

4. Desktop App Network Use and Diagnostics

4.1 Local Network

The Desktop App listens for racing simulation telemetry over UDP on the configured port (which defaults to 20777 where applicable). When pairing with the Android App is used, the Desktop App advertises itself on the local network through mDNS/DNS-SD and accepts local WebSocket connections from paired devices. This traffic stays on your local network.

4.2 Update Checks

Builds Electron and Qt. The Minimal build does not check for updates.
What happens At most once every 24 hours, on launch, the Desktop App asks GitHub's public API (api.github.com) for the version number of the latest Track N Race release and tells you if a newer one is available.
Data handling The request does not include telemetry, settings, recordings, or any identifier created by the Desktop App. As with any internet request, GitHub receives your IP address and standard request information, which GitHub handles under its own privacy statement. The developer does not receive this request.
Your choice Update checks can be turned off in the Desktop App's settings.

4.3 Diagnostic Logs and Crash Dumps

To help troubleshoot problems, the Desktop App writes diagnostic logs, and the Electron build also records crash dumps, to its application data folder on your computer. Logs may include the application and operating system versions, platform and architecture, system locale, file paths, and the addresses of your computer's network interfaces.

These files are kept on your computer and are not uploaded automatically. Launch diagnostics are replaced each time the Desktop App starts. They reach the developer only if you choose to send them, for example when reporting an issue.

5. Local Storage and Session Recordings

The Apps may offer an optional session recording feature, which is disabled by default where configured as described in the Apps.

When enabled, telemetry data may be saved in .tnrd files. On Android, these are saved in an application-accessible Documents directory or in a location selected by the user through the Android document picker. On desktop, they are saved in a folder you choose in the Desktop App.

Session recordings are stored locally unless you choose to move, export, or share them. The developer does not receive these recordings through a Track N Race-operated server.

You are responsible for managing exported recordings and any copies stored in other locations or shared with other parties.

6. The Website

6.1 Hosting

The Website is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the Website, Cloudflare processes the information that every web request carries, such as your IP address, browser user agent, and the page requested. Cloudflare handles this information under its own privacy policy and may make aggregated traffic statistics available to us. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

6.2 Download and Event Counts

The Website counts clicks on its download buttons and openings of its download dialog using Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine, so that we can see which builds and platforms people use. Each count records only:

  • What was clicked: the build (Electron, Qt or Minimal), the release page, or the download dialog.
  • The platform: the operating system of the chosen download, or, for the download dialog, the operating system your browser reports (Windows, macOS or Linux).
  • The Website page the click came from.
  • Your country, as determined by Cloudflare from the request.
  • The current Track N Race release version.

No IP address, cookie, browser user agent, or other identifier is stored with these counts, and they cannot be linked to an individual visitor. Cloudflare retains this data for a limited period, currently three months.

6.3 Downloads

Download links on the Website redirect to the corresponding file on GitHub, which serves the download. GitHub handles that request under its own privacy statement.

6.4 Cookies and Third-Party Content

The Website does not set cookies, does not use browser storage to track visitors, and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or social media trackers. Its fonts, scripts, and images are served from the Website itself. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies if it needs to check a request for abuse.

The Website links to external sites, such as GitHub and Discord. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this Policy does not cover.

7. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

The Apps do not intentionally integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, behavioral tracking services, or crash-reporting services.

The Services rely on the following third parties in the limited ways described above:

GitHub
Hosts the source code and release downloads, and answers the Desktop App's update checks.
Cloudflare
Hosts the Website and stores its anonymous download and event counts.

This Privacy Policy does not claim to govern information collected independently by third-party websites, operating systems, network providers, or other services outside our control.

8. Disclosure and Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell, rent, or disclose personal information to third parties. The Apps are designed without a backend collection service, and the Website's download and event counts contain no personal information.

Information may be exchanged between your own compatible devices as necessary for the Apps' functionality. You may also choose to export, transfer, or share recordings, diagnostic files, or other locally stored files yourself.

Information that you voluntarily disclose or transfer outside the Apps may become subject to the privacy practices of the recipient or service involved.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

The Apps do not maintain a developer-operated server-side repository of users' telemetry, pairing information, or session recordings.

Locally stored information may be deleted or managed as follows:

Pairings
Use the Android App's "Forget Desktop" feature, or remove the paired device in the Desktop App, where available.
Session recordings
Delete .tnrd files through the Apps, a file manager, or the storage location in which they were saved.
Android App settings and local data
Clear the Android App's data through Android settings or uninstall the Android App, subject to Android's storage behavior and any files exported to other locations.
Desktop App settings, logs and local data
Uninstall the Desktop App and delete its application data folder. Launch diagnostics are also replaced automatically each time the Desktop App starts.
Website counts
Download and event counts are anonymous and cannot be linked to you, so they cannot be retrieved or deleted for an individual. Cloudflare deletes them at the end of its retention period.

Uninstalling an App or clearing its data is not guaranteed to delete files that you previously exported or saved outside the App's managed storage.

10. Security and Pairing Credentials

The Apps may store credentials used to reconnect paired devices.

In the Android App, the reconnection token is encrypted at rest using AES-GCM with a 256-bit key held in the Android Keystore system. The key never leaves the device, so although the Android App's preferences may be included in Android system backups, a backed-up or copied token cannot be decrypted or used on another device.

In the Desktop App, pairing secrets are protected with the operating system's credential storage where available: Windows Data Protection (DPAPI), the macOS Keychain (Electron build, once you opt in), or the Secret Service keyring on Linux. Where no such storage is available, they are kept in the Desktop App's local settings without this additional encryption.

Security depends on the device, operating system version, operating system integrity, and the actual implementation of the Apps. No method of local storage or electronic communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure under all circumstances.

You should keep your devices and operating systems up to date and protect access to them.

11. Children's Privacy

Track N Race does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

The Services are not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been inadvertently disclosed to the developer in connection with the Services, you may contact us using the details in Section 14.

12. Your Privacy Choices

You can exercise control over the information processed by the Services by:

  • Revoking Android App permissions through Android settings.
  • Turning off update checks in the Desktop App's settings.
  • Choosing whether to enable optional session recording.
  • Removing paired devices.
  • Deleting locally stored recordings, diagnostic files, and application data.
  • Choosing whether to export or share locally stored files.
  • Downloading releases directly from GitHub instead of through the Website, if you prefer your download not to be counted.

The availability of specific controls may vary by App version, build, and operating system.
